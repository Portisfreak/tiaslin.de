<p>The campaign world is a high medieval gothic fantasy setting.</p>
<p>Within the vast and powerful Valoran Empire, your journeys start in Hartmoor, a small barony within the Duchy of Arvannes, not too far from the border provinces of the Empire. Though not yet directly threatened by Goblin Attacks, the looming threat of war and violence sows unease across the people of the duchy, and Duchess <em>Éloane</em> of Arvannes must strive for Empire's support within the High Council of Aubescourt.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, another threat appears to be emerging in the Barony of Hartmoor: rumor speaks of a Covenant cult operating in the region, allegedly responsible for recent heresies and, quite possibly, for a string of murders that were recently committed.</p>
<p>This is your home, and your home is threatened by forces beyond your understanding. With the Goblin armies crossing the marches and false gods' servants looming over your community, the burden falls to you to stand fast and hold the line.</p>

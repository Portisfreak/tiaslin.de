<p>A player&rsquo;s guide to the ways of life open to your character in the world of <em>Deliver Us from Evil</em>.</p>

<p class="open-question">Our game runs on the <em>Tales of the Valiant</em> rules, so all references are made to the <em>Tales of the Valiant</em> Player's Guide and Player's Guide 2</p>

<h3>The Martial Paths</h3>

<h4>Fighter</h4>
<p>The backbone of every army, watch, and caravan guard in the realm. Fighters study war the way clerics study scripture — with devotion. Where others pray or bargain, the fighter answers with steel, discipline, and ten thousand hours of practice.</p>
<ul class="concept-list">
  <li><strong>Weapon Master</strong> — The pure professional. One weapon becomes a language you speak fluently; masteries, precision, and the patient craft of a lifetime at the drills.</li>
  <li><strong>Scrapper</strong> — The grappler. Shove, throw, and pin — the close-quarters brawler who ends arguments at arm&rsquo;s length, which is exactly as far as they need.</li>
  <li><strong>Stunt Archer</strong> — The trick-shot. Arrows around corners, off helmets, through the impossible gap. The kind of archer crowds gather to watch.</li>
  <li><strong>Twinblade</strong> — The dual-wielder. Two edges, one blur — a mobile, whirling style that trades armor for angles.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Rogue</h4>
<p>The world is not kind, and rogues learned that early. They are the shadow-economists of civilization: lockpicks, silver tongues, and knives where you didn&rsquo;t expect them. Not all are criminals — some are investigators, scouts, enforcers, spies. All of them see the angles.</p>
<ul class="concept-list">
  <li><strong>Thief</strong> — The classic. Fast hands, faster feet, and an uncanny instinct for the exact moment no one is watching.</li>
  <li><strong>Enforcer</strong> — The leg-breaker. Muscle with rogue training: ambush instincts, a brawler&rsquo;s patience, and the first hit that ends most conversations.</li>
  <li><strong>Trapsmith</strong> — The gadgeteer. Wires, springs, and patient, spiteful ingenuity. The trap you didn&rsquo;t see was built by someone who thought about how you walk.</li>
</ul>

<div class="divider"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 12 12"><path d="M6 0 L12 6 L6 12 L0 6 Z" fill="currentColor"/></svg></div>

<h4>Barbarian</h4>
<p>Civilization is a thin crust, and barbarians remember what walks beneath it. Their fury is not technique — it is something older, and it comes out of the marrow, not the mind.</p>
<ul class="concept-list">
  <li><strong>Path of the Berserker</strong> — Fury without brakes. The body as a storm; the enemy as the shore.</li>
  <li><strong>Path of Wild Fury</strong> — The old ways. A beast&rsquo;s might channeled through markings, symbols, or tokens — tiger stripes tattooed, a bear-hide helm, a hawk-feather token. The village grandmother would call it a <em>kind</em> of faith. The Church has opinions on that.</li>
</ul>

<h3>The Old Paths — Primal Ways</h3>

<h4>Druid</h4>
<p>The world remembers what it was before walls and wards, and druids are the ones who still listen. Their magic is not granted by any altar, cannot be revoked by any bishopric, and needs no license — which is precisely what makes both churches nervous.</p>
<ul class="concept-list">
  <li><strong>Circle of the Leaf</strong> — The plant-speaker. Talks to the green world, calls groves into being, and is welcome in deep woods where armed men are not.</li>
  <li><strong>Circle of the Shifter</strong> — The shapeshifter. Wears the wild from the inside — not <em>mimicking</em> the beast, but borrowing the world&rsquo;s own shape.</li>
  <li><strong>Circle of the Fey</strong> — The fey-touched. Songs the woods answer to, and movement tricks that suggest the hedge-folk taught you personally.</li>
  <li><strong>Circle of the Stoneheart</strong> — The stone&rsquo;s memory. Earth, rock, and the deep patience of geology — occasionally thrown at problems.</li>
</ul>

<div class="divider"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 12 12"><path d="M6 0 L12 6 L6 12 L0 6 Z" fill="currentColor"/></svg></div>

<h4>Ranger</h4>
<p>Half fighter, half druid, entirely at home where the roads end. A ranger&rsquo;s magic is small, instinctive, and tangled up with survival itself — the way a hawk knows thermals, a ranger knows the wild.</p>
<ul class="concept-list">
  <li><strong>Hunter</strong> — The specialist of monsters. Knows what hunts in the dark, studies it, prepares for it, and then hunts it back.</li>
  <li><strong>Pack Master</strong> — The bonded. A spirit companion walks beside you — takes wounds meant for you, delivers your spells, and holds grudges on your behalf.</li>
  <li><strong>Trailblazer</strong> — The guide. Marks the quarry, opens the path, and turns a party of stumbling scholars into a hunting pack.</li>
</ul>

<h3>The Faithful — Divine Paths</h3>

<h4>Cleric</h4>
<p>The Light&rsquo;s answer to a dark world. Every cleric is an instrument of the Dawnward — healer, judge, soldier of the faith. Ordination is not a formality; it is a contract witnessed by heaven, and the Church keeps the paperwork.</p>
<ul class="concept-list">
  <li><strong>Life Domain</strong> — The shepherd of the natural cycle. Healing hands, mercy in field and hospice, and fierce opposition to the perversion of undeath.</li>
  <li><strong>Light Domain</strong> — Radiance, truth, the fire of the sun. The Light made weapon and witness. The most public face of the faith.</li>
  <li><strong>War Domain</strong> — The Church militant. Consecrated steel, marked foes, blessed wrath. The Light does not turn the other cheek at the marches.</li>
</ul>

<div class="divider"><svg width="12" height="12" viewBox="0 0 12 12"><path d="M6 0 L12 6 L6 12 L0 6 Z" fill="currentColor"/></svg></div>

<h4>Paladin</h4>
<p>Where the cleric is the trained instrument of the Church, the paladin is the oath itself made flesh. A paladin swears before a power that holds them to it — and that power answers. The oath is not a formality, and breaking it is not forgiven.</p>
<ul class="concept-list">
  <li><strong>Oath of Devotion</strong> — The knight in shining honesty. Duty, honor, justice — the paladin as the Light intends them: shield of the weak, sword against the dark.</li>
  <li><strong>Oath of Justice</strong> — The judge. Curses laid by right, judgement spoken with authority, and radiant sunlight that burns those who choose to stand in it.</li>
  <li><strong>Oath of Anathema</strong> — The monster-hunter. Sworn against the things that prey on the innocent — the specialist&rsquo;s oath, and the hunts begin at the altar.</li>
  <li><strong>Oath of Safekeeping</strong> — The bodyguard. Sworn to the one thing that cannot defend itself. The shield that arrives before the blow.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Whispers — The Other Paths</h3>
<p><em>Every village has a story about someone who made a bargain. The stories don&rsquo;t have happy endings, but they don&rsquo;t always have sad ones, either. There is no class for this. If it happens to your character, it will be because of something you did — a door you knocked on, a promise you made. Ask your GM no questions; the answers cost more than the asking.</em></p>

<div class="rule"><svg width="10" height="10" viewBox="0 0 10 10"><path d="M5 0 L10 5 L5 10 L0 5 Z" fill="currentColor"/></svg></div>
<p style="text-align:center; color: var(--ink-faint); font-style: italic;">Speak with your GM about which of these paths fits the story you want to tell — and remember: in this world, power always has a source, and the source always has terms.</p>

<div class="primer-frame">
  <p>Before the light, there was <strong>Silence</strong>, the age of darkness and despair. A savage age, marked by chaos and disorder, and civilization lay barren and bare. False gods reigned, we sacrificed our blood and souls to them, and were left in dirt and mud, no light to warm our hearts.</p>
  <p>A light whispered in the dark and silent. Yet so quiet, our torn hearts could not hear, but for one that wasn't broken.</p>
  <p>From the dark rose our savior, our Lady Lucienne, herald of Light, and turned whispers into shouts and cries for us to listen. She broke the silence and unveiled the false gods' lies. Their dark reign should end, and in their place the One True Light would shine.</p>
  <p>And thus dawned the age of light. Where there was dirt now orchards flower, cities sprawl, and where there was chaos, our Empire rose to protect what we have nurtured.</p>
  <p>Still, the dark prevails. Through witches' whispers, the false gods plot and scheme to douse the light that blinds them. Goblins cross the marches as their faithful servants to announce our desolation.</p>
  <p class="prayer">Oh Lady Lucienne, deliver us from evil. Keep with us the one true light, so we may illuminate the dark that lingers.</p>
</div>

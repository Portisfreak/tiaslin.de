<div class="lead">
  <p><strong>Faith is everywhere.</strong> Even the skeptic swears by the Dawnward when the wolves are at the door. Choose your relationship to it: devout, dutiful, doubting, or daring — but have one.</p>
  <p><strong>Magic is not casual.</strong> Miracles belong to the Church; everything else is a crime or a secret. Access is limited from the get go, and more often than not, magic comes as a threat rather than a savior.</p>
  <p><strong>The world is small and dark.</strong> It's as much grimdark as dark fantasy. It's dangerous and unforgiving; behind each smile might loom a hidden knife.</p>
</div>

<h3>The Valoran Empire</h3>
<p>Half the known continent, one crown. The Valoran Empire is the world's great success story: roads that run safe, walled towns, guilds and granaries, a fleet that rules its coastline. Its signature is <em>order</em> — the pax imperialis, a peace so real its people have forgotten it was ever otherwise. The imperial crown sits atop a feudal ladder of kings, dukes, and barons, all watched over by the great institutions of the High Council. Beyond the borders, the world is wilder: kingdoms where brigands roam and goblins still raid, and where the Empire's peace ends, its self-image begins.</p>

<h3>The Goblin Tribes</h3>
<p>Not bandits, not beasts — a people. The goblin tribes have sprawled across the Weald, an unmapped region beyond the marches, since before the Empire, kept survivable for centuries only by their endless feuds with one another. Now the tribes are uniting under a shaman-prophet's banner, settling the marches, and marching to war. Servants of the old gods, they do not come to conquer. They come to end the world of man.</p>

<h3>The Dawnward</h3>
<p>The Church of the One True Light, founded by the Lady Lucienne herself three centuries ago. It is the soul of the Empire: crowns the emperor, blesses the harvest, tends the poor through its Almoners — and hunts heresy through its Inquisition, for within the Empire the old faith is not only sin, but crime. Its priests carry the Divine, the only sanctioned magic in the world of man. For most folk, the Dawnward <em>is</em> goodness — and most of its faithful genuinely are.</p>

<h3>The Covenant</h3>
<p>The old faith — the pantheon that reigned during "the Silence". Within the Empire, its practice is outlawed and its name is a curse: witchcraft, devil worship, blood sacrifice. Yet, the old faith has survived: Household rites, oaths, seasonal feasts, and, in some communities, small covenants and sects are secretly practising the old rites where the Dawnward's eye can't reach.</p>
